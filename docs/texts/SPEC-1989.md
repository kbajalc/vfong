# Algorithmic Sequential Decision-Making in the Frequency Domain for Life Threatening Ventricular Arrhythmias and Imitative Artefacts: A Diagnostic System

**Authors:** S. Barro, R. Ruiz, D. Cabello, J. Mira
**Journal:** Journal of Biomedical Engineering, Vol. 11, July 1989, pp. 320–328
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/0141-5425(89)90067-8

---

## Abstract

A preliminary study to approach the problem of reliably detecting life threatening ventricular arrhythmias in real time is described. An algorithm (DIAGNOSIS) has been developed in order to classify ECG signal records on the basis of the computation of four simple parameters calculated from a representation in the frequency domain. This algorithm uses a set of rules constituting an operative classification scheme based on the comparison of the parameters with a set of pre-established thresholds. This allows differentiation of four general categories: ventricular fibrillation-flutter, ventricular rhythms, imitative artefacts and predominant sinus rhythm.

**Keywords:** ECG monitoring, ventricular arrhythmias, spectral analysis, clustering

---

## Introduction

VF is characterized in the frequency domain by a sinusoidal pattern with a narrow peak in the low frequency zone (4–9 Hz), contrasting with normal ECG which has a much wider frequency distribution. Imitative artefacts present distinguishable frequency-domain characteristics, suggesting preferential use of frequency domain techniques.

---

## ECG Records

- **Training set:** 55 records from CCU patients
  - 13: ventricular flutter or fibrillation (VF)
  - 15: rhythms with aberrant morphology (VT/CAM)
  - 15: imitative artefacts
  - 12: predominant sinus rhythm
- **Test set:** 39 records (none from training set)
  - 10 VF, 15 CAM, 14 artefacts
- Sampling rate: 200 Hz
- Segments: 1024 points (~5.12 s)

---

## Algorithm

### Preprocessing

1. Hamming window applied to 1024-point segment
2. Radix-2 FFT (32-bit Macroassembler)
3. Amplitude spectrum: modulus approximated as sum of |real| + |imaginary|
4. Maximum peak found in 0.5–9 Hz range
5. Components < 5% of maximum set to zero
6. Analysis range: 0–100 Hz (first 20 harmonics of reference frequency F); range 0–0.5 Hz excluded

### Spectral Features

Let F = reference frequency (dominant peak frequency for VF/artefacts, or heart rate for rhythms with identifiable complexes).

**FSMN** (First Spectral Moment Normalized) — center of gravity:
$$\text{FSMN} = \frac{1}{F} \frac{\sum x_i f_i}{\sum x_i}$$

Values near 1 indicate spectral concentration around F (characteristic of VF). Larger values indicate dispersion toward higher harmonics.

**A1** — ratio of area below F/2 to total area (0.5 Hz to 20F):
- High values indicate artefacts; discriminates VF from imitative artefacts.

**A2** — ratio of area in band [0.7F, 1.4F] to total area:
- High in VF category.

**A3** — ratio of area around harmonics 2–8 (bands of 0.6F width) to total area:
- High for sinus rhythm; very low for VF and artefacts.

### Decision Rules (DIAGNOSIS Algorithm)

VF is identified if: **FSMN ≤ 1.55**, **A1 > 0.19**, **A2 ≥ 0.45**, **A3 ≤ 0.09**

The algorithm uses sequential decision-making; not all four descriptors need be calculated for every record.

## Results

| Category | FP | FN | Specificity (%) | Sensitivity (%) |
|----------|----|----|-----------------|-----------------|
| VF       | 3  | 0  | 70              | 100             |
| CAM      | 0  | 3  | 100             | 86              |
| A        | 0  | 1  | 100             | 92              |

- VF sensitivity = 100% (10/10 correctly detected)
- VF specificity = 70% (3 false positives: 2 extreme CAM, 1 imitative artefact)
- The 3 FP cases were borderline; clinically appropriate to classify as VF given life-threatening presentation

---

## Conclusions

- Reliable detection of fast ventricular arrhythmias using frequency-domain spectral descriptors
- Sequential decision scheme; computationally efficient using FFT
- Designed as parallel processing block triggered by primary beat-to-beat processor
- High priority placed on VF sensitivity to minimize false negatives

## Key Spectral Characteristics by Category

| Category | FSMN | A1 | A2 | A3 |
|----------|------|----|----|----|
| VF/Flutter | ~1 (narrow peak at F) | Low | High (>0.45) | Very low |
| CAM (VT) | Moderate | Low | Moderate | Present up to 8th harmonic |
| Artefacts | Variable | **High** | Moderate | Low |
| Sinus rhythm | High (many harmonics) | Low | Low | High |
